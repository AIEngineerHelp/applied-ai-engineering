id: battery-switch
title: Engineering note: Atlas-3 battery change
kind: Engineering note
date: 2026-04-28
---

From the May build, the Atlas-3 uses the Sundra C2 cell from Sundra Power instead of the LX-4 lithium cell.

We made the change because the LX-4 lost a third of its capacity below minus ten degrees in the Osterby winter trials, which would have cut battery life to under three years for northern customers.

The Sundra C2 has the same footprint, so the main board does not change.
