title: Meridian flow meter: launch note
kind: Product brief
date: 2026-03-03

The Meridian flow meter is our new product for irrigation networks. It reports flow and pressure every five minutes through TGW, our telemetry gateway.

Meridian uses the same QX7 modem as the Atlas-3 for its cellular link. Its flow element is the FS-9 sensing element, supplied by Kerrin Sensors.

Meridian units are assembled at the Tartu plant, on the line next to the Atlas-3.
