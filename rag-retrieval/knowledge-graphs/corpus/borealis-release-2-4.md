date: 2026-02-24

Borealis 2.4 adds Modbus support. The Borealis controller now takes power from the PM-2 power module, which replaces the older PM-1.

The PM-2 power module is made by Varnell. Firmware updates for the controller are delivered through the Telemetry Gateway.

The controller design is maintained by T. Berg and the Hardware team. Borealis controllers are built at the Brno plant.
