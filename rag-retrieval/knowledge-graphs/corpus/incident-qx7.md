title: Incident review: QX-7 shipment delay
kind: Incident review
date: 2026-03-12
---

On 3 March 2026, Varnell Semiconductor told us that QX-7 shipments would slip by six weeks because of a wafer shortage.

The review recommended qualifying a second radio-chip supplier by the end of Q3 and keeping eight weeks of chip stock on hand.

No customer shipments were missed, because the warehouse held enough finished units to cover the gap.
