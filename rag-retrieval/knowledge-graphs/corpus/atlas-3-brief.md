The Atlas-3 is a battery-powered water-quality sensor. It sends readings every 15 minutes through the Telemetry Gateway.

The Atlas-3 main board uses the QX-7 radio chip for its cellular link. The housing is the IP68 enclosure, which is rated for continuous immersion to two metres.

The Atlas-3 runs on one LX-4 lithium cell. Battery life is rated at five years at the default reading interval.

Atlas-3 units are assembled at the Tartu plant.
