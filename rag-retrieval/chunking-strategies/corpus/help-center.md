# Tidewater Freight Help Center

Answers to the questions our customer support team hears most often. If you cannot find what you need, contact support from the Tidewater Console or call +1 503 555 0142, Monday to Friday, 6 am to 6 pm Pacific time.

## Account and billing

### How do I change the email address on my account?

Open Settings in the Tidewater Console, choose Profile, and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect only after you click it.

### Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, and ACH bank transfers for customers in the United States. Enterprise customers can also pay by wire transfer against a monthly invoice.

### When am I billed?

Starter and Growth plans are billed on the first day of each month for the previous month's shipments. Enterprise customers are invoiced monthly with net-30 payment terms.

### How do I download an invoice?

Go to Billing in the Console and choose Invoices. Each invoice can be downloaded as a PDF or a CSV with one line per shipment.

### Can I get a refund for a shipment I did not use?

Unused labels are not charged as long as you cancel them before the first carrier scan. Labels that were never cancelled and never scanned are voided automatically after 28 days and refunded to your account balance.

### How do I close my account?

Account owners can close an account from Settings, Account, Close account. Closing an account cancels any scheduled pickups, and we keep your shipment history for seven years to meet our record-keeping obligations.

## Shipping and pickups

### How do I schedule a pickup?

Choose Pickups in the Console, select the shipments to collect, and pick a date and a two-hour window. Pickups booked before 11 am local time can usually be collected the same day.

### Can I change a pickup after booking it?

You can move or cancel a pickup until 2 hours before the start of its window at no charge. Changes made later than that incur a $25 late-change fee.

### What happens if the driver arrives and my freight is not ready?

The driver waits up to 10 minutes. If the freight is still not ready, the pickup is marked as missed, a $35 missed-pickup fee is charged, and you will need to book a new pickup.

### Can I change an LTL freight pickup after booking it?

LTL freight pickups can be moved or cancelled until 5 pm local time on the business day before the pickup at no charge. Later changes incur a $75 late-change fee, because the carrier has already planned the truck route.

### What happens if the driver arrives and my LTL freight is not ready?

For LTL freight the driver waits up to 30 minutes. If the pallets are still not ready, the pickup is marked as missed and a $95 missed-pickup fee is charged, which covers the cost of the dispatched truck.

### What is the maximum weight for a parcel?

Ground and Express parcels can weigh up to 68 kg each. Anything heavier ships as LTL freight on a pallet.

### Do you ship hazardous materials?

We ship limited quantities of Class 3 flammable liquids and Class 9 lithium batteries by Ground only. All other hazardous materials classes are not accepted, and shipments found to contain them are returned at the sender's expense.

### Do you deliver on weekends?

Express shipments are delivered on Saturdays in 40 major metropolitan areas at no extra cost. Ground and LTL freight are delivered Monday to Friday only.

## Tracking and delivery

### Why has my tracking not updated?

Tracking updates when a package is scanned at a facility. Long linehaul legs between hubs can take up to 36 hours without a scan, which is normal and does not mean the shipment is lost.

### What does the status "exception" mean?

An exception means something has delayed the shipment, such as weather, a wrong address or damage. The tracking page shows the reason and the expected new delivery date, and our team contacts you if action is needed.

### Can I redirect a shipment to a different address?

You can redirect a domestic shipment while it is in transit from the shipment page in the Console, for a $12 fee. Redirects are not available for international shipments once they have been handed to customs.

### What is proof of delivery?

Proof of delivery is the record of when and where a shipment was delivered, with the recipient's signature or a photo of the package at the door. You can download it from the shipment page for 18 months after delivery.

### What happens if nobody is home to receive my shipment?

The driver leaves a notice and tries again the next business day. After three failed attempts the shipment is held at the nearest Tidewater hub for 5 business days, and then it is returned to the sender.

### Do you charge a fuel surcharge?

Yes. A fuel surcharge applies to every shipment and is updated every Monday based on the national average diesel price. The current percentage is shown on the Rates page in the Console.

## Claims and damage

### How long do I have to file a claim for a damaged domestic shipment?

For shipments within the United States, file a damage claim within 30 days of the delivery date. Include photos of the damaged item and the packaging, and keep the packaging until the claim is resolved.

### How long do I have to file a claim for a damaged international shipment?

For international shipments, file a damage claim within 21 days of the delivery date. Claims filed later than that cannot be accepted, because the Montreal and CMR conventions set short notice periods for international carriers.

### How long do I have to file a claim for a lost shipment?

A shipment is considered lost if it has not been delivered 15 days after its estimated delivery date. You can then file a loss claim within 60 days of the estimated delivery date, for domestic and international shipments alike.

### How much will I be reimbursed for a damaged or lost shipment?

Without declared value, our liability is limited to $100 per parcel or $0.50 per pound for LTL freight. If you declared a higher value when creating the shipment, we reimburse the lower of the declared value and the documented cost of the goods.

### How do I declare a higher value?

Set the declared value when you create the shipment, in the Console or with the `declared_value` field in the API. Declared value coverage costs $1.10 per $100 of value above the first $100, with a maximum declared value of $50,000 per shipment.

### How long does it take to resolve a claim?

We acknowledge every claim within 2 business days and resolve most claims within 15 business days. Approved reimbursements are credited to your account balance, or paid to your bank account on request.

## International shipping

### Which countries do you ship to?

We ship from the United States to Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom and all 27 member states of the European Union. Shipments to other countries are handled by partner carriers and are available to Enterprise customers only.

### Who pays duties and taxes?

By default, the recipient pays duties and taxes on delivery. You can choose Delivered Duty Paid when creating the shipment, and we will bill the duties and taxes to your account instead, plus a 2% handling fee.

### What documents do I need for an international shipment?

Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice listing each item, its value and its harmonized tariff code. The Console generates the commercial invoice for you from the shipment details.

### How long do international shipments take?

Express shipments to Canada and Mexico usually arrive in 2 to 3 business days, and to the United Kingdom and the European Union in 3 to 5 business days. Customs inspections can add 1 to 4 days.

### Can I change the delivery address of an international shipment?

Before the shipment reaches customs you can ask support to change the address for a $30 fee, and the commercial invoice is updated for you. After customs clearance has started, the address cannot be changed.

## Returns

### How do I create a return label?

Open the original shipment in the Console and choose Create return. The return label is valid for 30 days and is billed only when it is scanned.

### Can my customers create their own return labels?

Yes. Growth and Enterprise customers can enable the self-service returns portal, which lets recipients create a return label using their order number and postal code.

### How long do returns take to arrive?

Domestic returns ship by Ground and usually arrive in 3 to 7 business days. International returns are not supported through the returns portal; contact support to arrange them.

## Developers and integrations

### Where do I find my API key?

Open the Console, choose Developers, then API Keys. Keys starting with tw_test_ work only in the sandbox, and keys starting with tw_live_ work only in production.

### Do you have integrations with ecommerce platforms?

We offer native apps for Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce. The apps import orders automatically, buy labels at your negotiated rates and write tracking numbers back to the order.

### Why am I getting a 429 error?

A 429 response means your account has sent more requests than your plan allows in a one-minute window. Wait for the number of seconds in the Retry-After header before retrying, or upgrade your plan for higher limits.
