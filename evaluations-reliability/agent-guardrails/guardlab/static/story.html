The Gatekeepers of Lantern Mountain

An eager apprentice, a clever fox, a shopkeeper, and the five guards she sets up. Every trick in this story is a real attack on AI agents, and every number is measured.

Pip believes every scroll

Pip works the counter of Hara's mountain shop. Pip can look up orders, change them, send letters, and open the refund chest. Pip has one weakness: whatever a scroll says, Pip does.

In a real systemPip is an AI agent: a model with tools. Models can't reliably tell your instructions from instructions hidden in what they read.

Guard 3: the ledger lock

Hara puts a lock on the vault that ignores scrolls and reads only the ledger: whose order is it, when was it delivered, how much was refunded? It also stops requests that aren't tricks at all, just not allowed.
