      Behind the scenes

      What you just watched

      The idea comes from Andrej Karpathy's “LLM Wiki” (April 2026). Raw sources are never edited, the wiki belongs to the model, and a schema file tells it how to keep the wiki. Three operations keep it going: ingest, query and lint.

      Scripted The five sources, the pages the agent writes at each step, the captions and the example answers were written by hand to show what a careful model should produce. No model was called.

      Computed Everything derived from those pages is computed by tested code: the diffs, the links, the index and log, the lint findings, the BM25 rankings and the fact checks.

      The face-off is one small example: raw chunks, three questions and a lexical ranker. It shows the mechanism; it is not a benchmark. The article covers the trade-offs, the failure modes and when RAG is the better choice.
