# Help-centre FAQ: invoice exports (draft)

Draft by a contract writer, for review by Rui Costa. 5 May 2026.

**How do I export my invoices?**
Go to Billing, then Invoices, then Export. Pick a date range and we will email you a CSV.

**How often are exports generated?**
Exports are generated every hour.

**How long is my export kept?**
Your export is kept for 7 days. After that, request a new one.

**Is there a limit on the date range?**
You can export up to 24 months at a time.
