# Appointments

## Booking
Appointments are 20 minutes long. New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork. Same-day appointments are released at 8:00 am each weekday.

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment. A late cancellation or missed appointment may be charged a 40 dollar fee. The fee is waived for illness or emergencies.

## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of the medicines you take.

## Telehealth
Video visits are available for follow-ups, medication reviews and minor illnesses. They are booked the same way as in-person visits.
