# Billing and insurance

## Accepted insurance
The clinic accepts most major plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare and Medicare. Coverage for a specific plan should be confirmed with the billing office.

## Self-pay
A standard visit costs 120 dollars for patients without insurance. Payment plans are available through the billing office.

## Billing questions
Questions about a bill are handled by the billing office, open Monday to Friday from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
