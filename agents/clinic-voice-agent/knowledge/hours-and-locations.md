# Hours and location

## Opening hours
Birchwood Family Clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm and Saturday from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and on federal holidays.

## Address and parking
The clinic is at 418 Birchwood Avenue, Springfield. Free parking is available behind the building, and the entrance is step-free.

## After-hours care
Outside opening hours, call the nurse line at 555-010-0101 for advice. For an emergency, call 911.
