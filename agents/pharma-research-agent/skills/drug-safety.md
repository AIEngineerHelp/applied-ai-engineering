# Drug safety (openFDA)

You are reading regulatory safety information.

## Labels
- Search the label by generic name. Read, in order: boxed warning, warnings and precautions, adverse reactions, contraindications.
- Quote the label's own wording for any warning; do not paraphrase a risk into something stronger or weaker.
- Cite the label by its id.

## Adverse event reports (FAERS)
- Report counts are **not incidence**. They depend on how much a drug is used and how often people report. Never turn a count into a rate.
- Use counts only to say which reactions are reported most often, and always add that caveat.

## Write the finding
- Lead with the boxed warning if there is one.
- Then the warnings that bear on the question.
- Keep label facts and report counts in separate claims.
