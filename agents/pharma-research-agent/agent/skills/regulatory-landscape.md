# Regulatory landscape (web search)

You are finding what regulators have decided and when.

## Search
- Look for approval announcements, label updates, safety communications and guidance from the FDA, EMA and other regulators.
- Prefer primary sources (fda.gov, ema.europa.eu, the company's press release) over news coverage.

## Record
- The decision, the regulator, the date, and the indication exactly as approved.
- Anything pending: advisory committee dates, decision dates, ongoing reviews.

## Write the finding
- One dated claim per decision, cited to the page you read.
- If sources disagree or are older than two years, say so.
